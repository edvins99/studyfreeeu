Tuition-Free Universities in Romania

€0 for state-funded students; fees may be charged in fee-paying places

Tuition rules for EU citizens

In Romania, higher education is free of charge for state-funded students; institutions may collect other fees in defined situations, and fee-paying places exist. State-funded places are open to EU/EEA citizens on the same basis as Romanian citizens. Amounts for fee-paying places vary by institution.

Public universities

Free for state-funded students; fee-paying places otherwise.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 in state-funded places.
  • Master's: €0 in state-funded places.
  • PhD: Often funded for state-funded places.

EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
