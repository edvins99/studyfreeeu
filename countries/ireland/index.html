Tuition-Free Universities in Ireland

€0 tuition under Free Fees Initiative; student contribution ≈ €2,500–€3,000/year

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Eligible EU/EEA students in Ireland do not pay tuition under the Free Fees Initiative, but must pay a student contribution charge (€2,500 for 2025/26). Eligibility requires qualifying residency conditions (for example, time spent in EU/EEA/UK education).

Public universities

Tuition covered by Free Fees Initiative; student contribution payable.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Tuition-free if eligible; student contribution payable.
  • Master's: Free Fees generally applies to undergraduate; postgraduate fees vary.
  • PhD: Postgraduate fees vary; some funding available.

English-taught programmes

Predominantly English-taught; a large catalogue for international students.

Mandatory fees

Student contribution charge of €2,500 for 2025/26 (may rise), payable by eligible students.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €2,500.

Admission system & application process

CAO (Central Applications Office) for undergraduate; direct for postgraduate.

Residency conditions

Free Fees eligibility depends on EU/EEA/UK nationality plus residency/education history criteria.

Language requirements

English-taught; require English proof for non-native speakers.

EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
