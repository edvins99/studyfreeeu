Tuition-Free Universities in Denmark

⭐ Nordic focus country

€0 tuition for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Higher education at Danish public universities is free for students from the EU/EEA and Switzerland, at Bachelor's and Master's level. Non-EU/EEA students pay tuition of roughly €6,000–€16,000 per year.

Public universities

Tuition-free for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens.

Private universities

A small number of private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA.
  • Master's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA.
  • PhD: Doctoral positions are salaried and free of tuition.

English-taught programmes

Large English-taught catalogue, especially at Master's level.

Mandatory fees

No tuition; some institutions apply small administrative or student organisation fees.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: Not yet verified.

Admission system & application process

Direct university portals; optagelse.dk for some Bachelor's programmes.

Residency conditions

Citizenship-based exemption for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens.

Language requirements

English-taught programmes require English proof (commonly IELTS 6.5); Danish-taught programmes require Danish proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€900–€1,300 / month
Student housing
Student housing via municipalities and student organisations; apply early, especially in Copenhagen.
Scholarships
Danish government and university scholarships exist; EU/EEA pay no tuition.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
