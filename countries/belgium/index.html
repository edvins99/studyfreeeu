Tuition rules for EU citizens

Belgian public universities charge relatively low tuition, set separately by the Flemish Community and the Wallonia-Brussels Federation. For 2026-2027 the Flemish rate for a full-time EU/EEA degree programme is about €1,181.40 per year. In the Wallonia-Brussels Federation, EU nationals pay a maximum of about €1,194 per year, with reduced categories paying roughly €175–€835.

Public universities

Low tuition for EU/EEA; the exact amount depends on the Community (Flanders or Wallonia-Brussels) and the institution.

Private universities

A limited private sector charges higher fees.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Low tuition (EU/EEA rate) — see the relevant Community rate.
  • Master's: Low tuition (EU/EEA rate); some advanced Master’s may differ.
  • PhD: Doctoral candidates are usually salaried or funded.

English-taught programmes

Growing English-taught catalogue, especially at Master’s level.

Mandatory fees

No large separate mandatory semester fee; small administrative charges may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €1,194.

Admission system & application process

Direct institution portals; some programmes use joint enrolment procedures.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA students are charged the standard (lower) rate; non-EU/EEA rates are higher and vary by programme.

Language requirements

Programmes are taught in Dutch, French or English; language proof required accordingly.

EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
