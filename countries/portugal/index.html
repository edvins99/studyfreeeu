Tuition-Free Universities in Portugal

Capped low tuition: reference fee €697/year (Bachelor’s, 2025/26); up to €2,750 (Master’s)

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Portuguese public universities charge a capped maximum tuition (propina) set nationally and indexed annually. For 2025/2026 the reference fee was €697 for Bachelor’s degree programmes and up to €2,750 for Master’s. EU/EEA students are charged the same as Portuguese students. Amounts are verified per institution and year.

Public universities

Capped low tuition; EU/EEA charged national rates.

Private universities

Private institutions charge higher tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Capped low tuition (≈ €697/yr reference, 2025/26).
  • Master's: Capped low tuition (up to ≈ €2,750/yr, 2025/26).
  • PhD: Often funded via FCT scholarships.

English-taught programmes

A growing English-taught catalogue, especially at Master’s level.

Mandatory fees

Small enrolment/administrative fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €697.

Admission system & application process

National contest (CNA) and direct institution portals.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA students pay national tuition rates.

Language requirements

Portuguese-taught programmes require Portuguese; English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€600–€1,000 / month
Student housing
Student residences and shared flats; Lisbon and Porto are competitive.
Scholarships
FCT scholarships for research; university merit awards.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
