Tuition-Free Universities in Slovakia

€0 for programmes taught in Slovak (public universities); English programmes fee-based

Tuition rules for EU citizens

At Slovak public universities, study is free of charge when the programme is taught in Slovak; English-taught programmes are fee-based (commonly €1,000–€3,000 per year at public institutions). EU/EEA students benefit from the same language-based rule as Slovak students.

Public universities

Free in Slovak; fee-based in English.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 in Slovak; fee-based in English.
  • Master's: €0 in Slovak; fee-based in English.
  • PhD: Often free and sometimes salaried.

English-taught programmes

A moderate English-taught catalogue, mostly fee-based.

Mandatory fees

Small administrative fees may apply.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€500–€800 / month
Student housing
Dormitories (študentské domovy) are cheap; Bratislava is competitive.
Scholarships
Slovak government scholarships (SAIA); university support.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
