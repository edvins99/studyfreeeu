Tuition-Free Universities in Austria

€0 tuition for regular study duration; conditional fee afterwards

Tuition rules for EU citizens

EU/EEA (and Swiss) citizens pay no tuition at public universities as long as they study within the minimum duration plus two semesters. Beyond that, a fee of €363.36 per semester applies. All students pay the mandatory ÖH students' union fee.

Public universities

Tuition-free for EU/EEA within regular duration; ÖH fee always payable.

Private universities

Private universities charge tuition (often €2,000–€25,000 per year).

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA within regular duration; ÖH fee applies.
  • Master's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA within regular duration; ÖH fee applies.
  • PhD: €0 tuition for EU/EEA within regular duration; ÖH fee applies.

English-taught programmes

Many English-taught Master's programmes; fewer English-taught Bachelor's programmes (more common at private universities).

Mandatory fees

ÖH students' union fee of approximately €24.70 per semester (indexed annually). A conditional tuition fee of €363.36 per semester applies to EU/EEA students exceeding minimum duration + 2 semesters.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €50.

Admission system & application process

Direct university portals; many institutions support the joint Austrian application system for some programmes.

Residency conditions

No residency requirement. Non-EU/EEA students pay €726.72 per semester from the first semester, unless covered by an exemption.

Language requirements

German-taught programmes require German (commonly level C1 or a recognised diploma). English-taught programmes set their own English requirements.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€900–€1,300 / month
Student housing
Student halls (Studentenheime) and shared flats; the OeAD provides housing services.
Scholarships
See OeAD grants and university scholarships; the ÖH advises students.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
