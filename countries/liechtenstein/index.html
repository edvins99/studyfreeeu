Tuition-Free Universities in Liechtenstein

Low tuition: CHF 950/semester for EU/EEA and Swiss nationals (≈ CHF 1,900/yr)

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Liechtenstein is in the EEA but not the EU. Its University of Liechtenstein charges CHF 950 per semester for Bachelor’s, Master’s and Doctoral programmes to EU/EEA and Swiss nationals (about CHF 1,900 per year). Non-EU/EEA students pay higher fees.

Public universities

Low tuition (CHF 950/semester for EU/EEA & Swiss nationals).

Private universities

Very limited provision.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: CHF 950/semester (EU/EEA & Swiss).
  • Master's: CHF 950/semester (EU/EEA & Swiss).
  • PhD: CHF 950/semester (EU/EEA & Swiss).

English-taught programmes

Programmes mainly in German, some in English.

Mandatory fees

Small administrative fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €2,000.

Direct university portal (uni.li).

Residency conditions

EEA citizen treatment; non-EU/EEA pay higher fees.

Language requirements

Mainly German; some English; proof required accordingly.

EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
