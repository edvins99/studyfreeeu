Tuition-Free Universities in Estonia

€0 in Estonian-language study places; fee-based in English

Tuition rules for EU citizens

In Estonia, EU/EEA citizens can study free of charge in Estonian-taught programmes when admitted to a state-commissioned (free) study place. English-taught programmes generally charge tuition for all students, including EU/EEA citizens, in the range of roughly €1,660–€7,500 per year.

Public universities

Free in Estonian study places; English programmes are typically fee-based.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 in Estonian study places; fees in English.
  • Master's: €0 in Estonian study places; fees in English.
  • PhD: Doctoral study is often free and salaried.

English-taught programmes

A strong English-taught catalogue at Master's level, mostly fee-based; some scholarships available.

Mandatory fees

No tuition in Estonian study places; small administrative fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: Not yet verified.

Admission system & application process

National admission via SAIS (sais.ee) and direct institution portals.

Residency conditions

Condition is language of instruction and study-place type, not nationality.

Language requirements

Estonian-taught programmes require Estonian (often B2); English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€500–€800 / month
Student housing
Dormitories available; Tallinn and Tartu markets are competitive.
Scholarships
Estonian government and university scholarships; some programmes offer tuition waivers.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
