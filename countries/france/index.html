Tuition-Free Universities in France

National rate: €178/year Bachelor's, €254/year Master's

Tuition rules for EU citizens

France charges low national tuition rates at public universities. For 2025-2026 these were €178 per year for a Licence (Bachelor's) and €254 per year for a Master's. Non-EU students from outside the EU/EEA may be charged differentiated (higher) rates unless exempted. Doctoral tuition is very low.

Public universities

Low national tuition rates for EU/EEA students.

Private universities

Private and some grande école programmes charge higher fees.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: ≈ €178 per year (national rate).
  • Master's: ≈ €254 per year (national rate).
  • PhD: ≈ €391 per year (national rate).

English-taught programmes

Growing English-taught catalogue, especially at Master's level.

Mandatory fees

Additional contributions such as the CVEC campus contribution (around €100–€175 per year) may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €400.

Admission system & application process

Parcoursup (Bachelor's) and Mon Master / Études en France for international applicants.

Residency conditions

Differentiated fees may apply to non-EU/EEA students; EU/EEA citizens pay the national rate.

Language requirements

French-taught programmes require French (commonly B2 via DELF/DALF/TCF); English programmes require English proof.

Deadlines & document submission

Parcoursup ≈ January; Mon Master ≈ February

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€800–€1,300 / month
Student housing
CROUS student residences are cheap but oversubscribed; private rentals are common.
Scholarships
Eiffel Excellence and Campus France scholarships; low national tuition for EU/EEA.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
