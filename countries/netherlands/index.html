Tuition-Free Universities in Netherlands

Statutory fee ≈ €2,694 for 2026-2027 (EU/EEA)

Tuition rules for EU citizens

EU/EEA students pay the government-set statutory tuition fee at Dutch public universities: €2,694 for the 2026-2027 academic year for full-time study. Non-EU/EEA students pay the higher institutional fee.

Public universities

Statutory fee applies for EU/EEA.

Private universities

Private institutions set their own fees.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Statutory fee applies.
  • Master's: Statutory fee applies (some programmes are institutional-fee only).
  • PhD: PhD candidates are usually employed and pay no tuition.

English-taught programmes

Very large English-taught catalogue at Bachelor's and Master's level.

Mandatory fees

No separate mandatory semester fee beyond the statutory tuition fee; some institutions add small administrative costs.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €2,694.

Admission system & application process

Direct university portals and Studielink (national enrolment).

Residency conditions

Statutory fee depends on nationality/residency conditions (EU/EEA/Swiss or qualifying residence permit).

Language requirements

English-taught programmes require English proof; Dutch-taught programmes require Dutch proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€900–€1,300 / month
Student housing
Housing is scarce — start very early; student housing corporations plus the private market.
Scholarships
Holland Scholarship and university awards for non-EU; EU/EEA pay the statutory fee.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
