Tuition-Free Universities in Sweden

⭐ Nordic focus country

€0 tuition for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens

Tuition rules for EU citizens

EU/EEA and Swiss citizens do not pay application or tuition fees at Swedish universities for Bachelor's or Master's studies. Non-EU/EEA students pay tuition (average around SEK 129,000 per year).

Public universities

Tuition-free for EU/EEA; a small mandatory student union fee at some institutions.

Private universities

A few private institutions exist (e.g. in business or design) and may charge fees.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA.
  • Master's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA.
  • PhD: Doctoral education is normally free and often salaried.

English-taught programmes

Very large English-taught catalogue, especially at Master's level.

Mandatory fees

Most universities charge a small student union fee (roughly SEK 50–350 per semester); no tuition and no application fee for EU/EEA applicants.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €25.

Admission system & application process

universityadmissions.se (national joint application).

Residency conditions

Citizenship-based exemption for EU/EEA/Swiss citizens.

Language requirements

English-taught programmes require English proof (commonly English 6 / IELTS 6.5); Swedish-taught programmes require Swedish proof.

Deadlines & document submission

Applications open
≈ mid-October
Applications close
15 January (autumn) / 15 September (spring)
Document submission
Upload documents to universityadmissions.se; certified copies/translations for some.
Note
National joint application; late applications only if places remain.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€800–€1,200 / month
Student housing
Housing via universities/student unions and nations; queues can be long in Uppsala, Lund and Stockholm.
Scholarships
Swedish Institute scholarships target international students; EU/EEA pay no tuition.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
