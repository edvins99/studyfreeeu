Tuition-Free Universities in Latvia

€0 in state-funded places (limited); most study places are fee-paying

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Latvia has a limited number of state-funded study places in which EU/EEA citizens may study free of charge; these are awarded on merit. Most students study in fee-paying places, with fees ranging widely (commonly from about €2,800 up to €15,000 per year, depending on the programme). Because only a minority receive free places, “conditional tuition-free” is the accurate description.

Public universities

Free only in state-funded places; otherwise fee-paying.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 in state-funded places; otherwise fee-paying.
  • Master's: €0 in state-funded places; otherwise fee-paying.
  • PhD: Often funded for state-funded places.

English-taught programmes

A moderate English-taught catalogue; English-taught programmes are generally fee-paying (state-funded places are mostly Latvian-taught).

Mandatory fees

Small administrative fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: Not yet verified.

Admission system & application process

Unified bachelor admission (VUPP, vienotauznemsana.lv) for most public universities — 2026 main round 9–20 July, results 27 July, €45 registration fee; other programmes (incl. most English-taught and arts/music academies) via direct institution portals.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA citizens may compete for state-funded (free) places on the same basis as Latvian citizens.

Language requirements

Latvian-taught programmes require Latvian; English programmes require English proof.

Living costs (approx.)
€450–€800 / month
Student housing
Dormitories available; the Riga market is competitive.
Scholarships
Latvian state scholarships; university support.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
