Tuition-Free Universities in Hungary

€0 in state-funded (scholarship) places; fee-based in self-financed places

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Hungarian public universities offer a number of state-funded (tuition-free) study places, awarded on merit, which EU/EEA citizens may take up on the same basis as Hungarian citizens. Other students study in self-financed, fee-paying places. The Stipendium Hungaricum programme provides additional fully funded scholarships.

Public universities

Free in state-funded places; fees in self-financed places.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Free if admitted to a state-funded place.
  • Master's: Free if admitted to a state-funded place.
  • PhD: Doctoral schools often include state scholarships.

English-taught programmes

A large English-taught catalogue, frequently fee-based.

Mandatory fees

No tuition in state-funded places; small administrative costs may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: Not yet verified.

Admission system & application process

Direct institution portals; Stipendium Hungaricum via the national scholarship system.

Residency conditions

State-funded places are merit-based and open to EU/EEA applicants on equal terms.

Language requirements

Hungarian-taught programmes require Hungarian; English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€450–€750 / month
Student housing
Dormitories and private rentals; Budapest is competitive.
Scholarships
Stipendium Hungaricum (fully funded) and state-funded (tuition-free) places.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
