Tuition rules for EU citizens

Study at public universities is free of charge for all students, including international students, when the programme is taught in Czech and studied within standard duration. Programmes taught in English (and other foreign languages) are subject to tuition fees.

Public universities

Free in the Czech language; English-taught programmes are fee-based.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 in Czech; fees in English.
  • Master's: €0 in Czech; fees in English.
  • PhD: Often free and sometimes salaried; some English-taught doctoral programmes are fee-based.

English-taught programmes

Over 1,000 programmes in English; these generally carry tuition fees.

Mandatory fees

No tuition for Czech-taught programmes; small administrative/application fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: Not yet verified.

Admission system & application process

Direct university portals; many offer a one-year Czech-language preparatory programme.

Residency conditions

Condition is language of instruction and standard duration, not nationality.

Language requirements

Czech-taught programmes require Czech (often B2 or a preparatory year); English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€600–€900 / month
Student housing
Dormitories (koleje) are affordable; Prague is competitive.
Scholarships
Government scholarships for Czech-language study; university merit scholarships.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
