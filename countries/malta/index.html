Tuition-Free Universities in Malta

€0 for EU students in most full-time programmes (specific course/bench fees may apply)

Tuition rules for EU citizens

The University of Malta does not charge standard tuition to Maltese and EU students in most full-time programmes; non-EU students pay fees. The university nevertheless publishes a separate fee schedule for EU/EEA students (covering certain courses, bench fees and international collaborative programmes), so small charges may still apply depending on the programme.
