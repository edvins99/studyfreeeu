Tuition-Free Universities in Italy

Income-based tuition: public universities ≈ €900–€4,000/year (EU/EEA may pay less)

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Italian public universities charge tuition that is largely income-based (progressive ISEE system), so many students pay low fees and top performers may be fully exempt. EU/EEA students are generally treated on the same basis as Italian students. Public-university tuition is commonly cited at roughly €900–€4,000 per year; private institutions are far higher.

Public universities

Income-based tuition; ranges commonly cited at €900–€4,000 per year.

Private universities

Private institutions charge higher tuition (€6,000–€20,000+ per year).

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Income-based; verify per institution.
  • Master's: Income-based; verify per institution.
  • PhD: Doctoral programmes are often funded with scholarships.

English-taught programmes

A large English-taught catalogue, especially at Master’s level.

Mandatory fees

Regional tax and stamp duties add small mandatory amounts; income-based tuition is the main charge.

Admission system & application process

National portal (Universitaly) and direct institution portals.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA students are generally charged the same tuition as Italian students (income-based).

Language requirements

Italian-taught programmes require Italian; English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€700–€1,200 / month
Student housing
Student residences (case dello studente) and private rentals; major cities are expensive.
Scholarships
Income-based fee reductions, DSU regional scholarships and the “Invest Your Talent in Italy” programme.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
