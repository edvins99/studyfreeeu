Tuition-Free Universities in Spain

Low public tuition: Bachelor’s ≈ €700–€1,700/yr; Master’s ≈ €1,000–€3,500/yr

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Spanish public universities charge low tuition, set by each autonomous community, with fairly wide variation. The European Commission cites Bachelor’s degrees at roughly €700–€1,700 per academic year and Master’s programmes at roughly €1,000–€3,500. EU/EEA students pay the same rates as Spanish students.

Public universities

Low tuition; set by autonomous community.

Private universities

Private institutions charge higher tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: ≈ €700–€1,700/yr; varies by region and institution.
  • Master's: ≈ €1,000–€3,500/yr; varies by region and institution.
  • PhD: Often funded via scholarships.

English-taught programmes

A growing English-taught catalogue, especially at Master’s level.

Mandatory fees

Small administrative and insurance fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €1,200.

Admission system & application process

Regional university admission systems and direct institution portals.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA students pay national/regional rates.

Language requirements

Spanish-taught programmes require Spanish (commonly B2); English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€700–€1,100 / month
Student housing
Student residences and shared flats; Madrid and Barcelona are competitive.
Scholarships
Spanish government, regional and university scholarships; income-based aid.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
