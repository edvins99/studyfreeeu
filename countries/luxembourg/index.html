Tuition-Free Universities in Luxembourg

Low tuition: ≈ €800/year (≈ €400/semester); some programmes free

Tuition rules for EU citizens

The University of Luxembourg charges low tuition. Most Bachelor’s and Master’s programmes fall in a low range (with some reported as free of charge), and EU/EEA students typically pay around €800 per year (about €400 per semester). Amounts vary by programme.

Public universities

Low tuition; EU/EEA rates are the same as national rates.

Private universities

Very limited private provision.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: Low tuition (≈ €400/semester); some programmes free — verify per programme.
  • Master's: Low tuition; some programmes free — verify per programme.
  • PhD: Doctoral candidates are usually salaried.

English-taught programmes

Multilingual teaching (English/French/German); many Master’s programmes in English.

Mandatory fees

Small administrative fees may apply.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €800.

Admission system & application process

Direct university portal.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA students pay the standard (low) rate.

Language requirements

Programmes are taught in English, French or German; proof required accordingly.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€1,200–€1,800 / month
Student housing
Very limited and expensive housing — apply immediately after admission.
Scholarships
Government study aid; some university scholarships.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.
