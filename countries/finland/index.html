Tuition-Free Universities in Finland

⭐ Nordic focus country

€0 tuition for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens

Tuition rules for EU citizens

EU/EEA and Swiss citizens are not liable for tuition fees at Finnish universities and universities of applied sciences, including English-taught degree programmes. Non-EU/EEA students (with exceptions) pay fees of roughly €8,000–€20,000 per year. Doctoral studies remain free of charge.

Public universities

Tuition-free for EU/EEA; a compulsory student union / student services fee applies.

Private universities

Finland's higher education sector is publicly funded; only a few private institutions operate, mainly in business and design.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA; student union fee applies.
  • Master's: €0 tuition for EU/EEA; student union fee applies.
  • PhD: Free of charge for all students.

English-taught programmes

One of Europe's largest English-taught catalogues at Bachelor's and Master's level.

Mandatory fees

Compulsory student union fee of roughly €60–€120 per year (varies between universities; includes student health care), plus a small one-off registration/administrative fee at some institutions.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: €80.

Admission system & application process

Studyinfo (opintopolku.fi) national joint application; some Master's admissions are direct.

Residency conditions

Citizenship-based: EU/EEA/Swiss citizens are exempt. From 2025, applicants from outside the EU/EEA/Switzerland pay a €100 application fee for Bachelor's programmes.

Language requirements

English-taught programmes require English proof (commonly IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 92, set per programme); Finnish/Swedish-taught programmes require national language proof.

Deadlines & document submission

Applications open
Early January
Applications close
≈ 22 January (joint application, autumn intake)
Document submission
Upload certificates to the Studyinfo application; certified copies may be requested later.
Note
Some Master’s programmes have separate, later rounds.

Typical dates for the 2026/2027 cycle — verify the exact deadline on the official admissions page.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€700–€1,300 / month
Student housing
Student housing foundations (e.g. HOAS in Helsinki) offer affordable rooms — apply early.
Scholarships
Many English-taught programmes offer merit scholarships; EU/EEA students pay no tuition.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
