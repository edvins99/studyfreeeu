Tuition-Free Universities in Slovenia

€0 tuition for full-time study for EU citizens

Tuition rules for EU citizens

At Slovenian public universities, full-time Bachelor's and Master's studies are free of tuition for Slovenian citizens and EU/EEA citizens (generally also for other eligible categories). Part-time and some English-taught programmes may be fee-based.

Public universities

Tuition-free for full-time study for eligible students; small enrolment fee may apply.

Private universities

Private institutions charge tuition.

By degree level

  • Bachelor's: €0 for full-time study (eligible students).
  • Master's: €0 for full-time study (eligible students).
  • PhD: Often free or low-cost; verify per institution.

English-taught programmes

A moderate English-taught catalogue; some English programmes are fee-based.

Mandatory fees

Small enrolment/administration fee may apply for candidates who have already completed the same level of education.

Estimated mandatory university cost per year: Not yet verified.

Admission system & application process

National application portal (eVŠ) for entry to study, plus direct institution portals.

Residency conditions

EU/EEA citizens are treated on the same basis as Slovenian citizens for full-time study.

Language requirements

Slovene-taught programmes require Slovene; English programmes require English proof.

Living costs, housing & scholarships

Living costs (approx.)
€600–€900 / month
Student housing
Subsidised student dormitories (študentski domovi) — apply early, especially in Ljubljana.
Scholarships
Slovenian government scholarships (bilateral) and university support.
EU/EEA mobility
As an EU/EEA citizen you do not need a visa or residence permit to study here; register with the local authorities after arrival. Non-EU/EEA students need a visa/residence permit.

Living-cost figures are approximate (2026 estimates) and separate from the mandatory university cost above.
