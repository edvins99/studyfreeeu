Tuition-Free Universities in Switzerland

Low tuition: cantonal universities ≈ CHF 1,000–1,500/yr (Swiss/EU); ETH/EPFL higher for foreign students

Tuition rules for EU citizens

Switzerland is not an EU member but has close academic ties. Cantonal universities charge low tuition (commonly around CHF 1,000–1,500 per year for Swiss/EU students). The two federal institutes (ETH Zurich and EPFL) charge higher fees to foreign students — reported at CHF 2,190 per semester from autumn 2025. Amounts vary by canton and institution and must be verified directly.

Public universities

Low tuition set by canton; EU/EEA rates vary.

  • Master's: Low cantonal tuition; verify per institution.
