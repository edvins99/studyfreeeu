16 terms explained

Glossary

Quick definitions of the fees, documents and terms you’ll meet when applying to a European university.

Degrees

ECTS

European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System — a common credit system. A full academic year is typically 60 ECTS (Bachelor’s ≈ 180, Master’s ≈ 120).

Degrees

Bachelor / Master / PhD

The three-cycle degree structure: undergraduate (first cycle), postgraduate (second cycle) and doctoral (third cycle).

Fees

Semester fee (Semesterbeitrag)

A mandatory contribution many “tuition-free” universities charge per semester, covering administration, student services and often a public-transport ticket.

Fees

Students’ union fee (ÖH fee)

The compulsory Austrian students’ union contribution (approx. €24.70 per semester) paid even when tuition is €0.

Fees

CVEC

The French “Contribution Vie Étudiante et de Campus”, a small mandatory campus-life contribution added to tuition.

Fees

ISEE

The Italian income indicator used to calculate income-based university tuition.

Fees

Propina

The Portuguese term for the maximum annual public-university tuition fee, set nationally and indexed.

Eligibility

State-funded place

A government-funded study place (used in the Nordics, Baltics, Czechia, Poland, Hungary, Romania, etc.) that is free of tuition and usually allocated by merit.

Eligibility

Conditional tuition-free

Free only under a condition — most often the language of instruction (study in the national language) or staying within the regular study duration.

Documents

Nostrification

The recognition of a foreign school-leaving or higher-education qualification by the host country’s authorities (used in Czechia, Slovakia, etc.).

Documents

Legalisation / apostille

Official confirmation that a document is authentic, often required for foreign certificates (e.g. Poland, Bulgaria).

Documents

Declaration of value / CIMEA

Italian mechanisms to assess and compare a foreign qualification.

Documents

uni-assist

A central service used by many German universities to check international credentials before admission.

Eligibility

EEA

European Economic Area — the EU plus Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway. EEA and Swiss citizens share the EU tuition rules described here.

Degrees

Erasmus+

The EU programme supporting study, training and exchanges across European countries.

Degrees

Academic year 2026/2027

The academic year in focus in this database; some national sources still quote 2025/2026 until the authorities update them.
