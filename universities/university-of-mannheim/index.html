Admissions & entry requirements

Who can apply

A recognised secondary school qualification for a Bachelor’s degree; a relevant Bachelor’s degree for a Master’s. Entry follows Germany admission rules.

Language requirements

German-taught programmes require proof of German (commonly DSH-2/TestDaF). English-taught programmes require English proof (IELTS/TOEFL), level set by each programme.

English test

Proof of English proficiency (e.g. IELTS/TOEFL) for English-taught programmes; threshold set per programme.

Documents generally required 4 items
  • Secondary school certificate + transcript
  • Bachelor’s degree + transcript (for Master’s)
  • Language certificate
  • Passport / ID copy

Application deadlines (typical)

15 July (winter semester) / 15 January (summer semester)

How to apply

uni-assist e.V. (for many universities) and direct university portals; DoSV/hochschulstart for some medicine/restricted programmes. · Admissions portal →
