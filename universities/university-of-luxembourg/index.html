Available specialities & what’s distinctive

Law Economics Computer Science Humanities Engineering Finance

Luxembourg’s only university (founded 2003), distinctive for multilingual teaching (English/French/German) and strength in law, finance and computer science.

Admissions & entry requirements

Who can apply

A secondary school leaving certificate giving access to higher education. For a Master’s: a relevant Bachelor’s degree.

Language requirements

Programmes are taught in English, French or German; language proof required accordingly.

English test

Proof of English proficiency (e.g. IELTS/TOEFL or equivalent); the exact threshold is set by the programme.

Documents generally required

  • Secondary school certificate
  • Bachelor’s degree + transcript (for Master’s)
  • Language certificate
  • Passport / ID copy

Application deadlines (typical)

Typical: applications ≈ spring for the autumn intake; verify on the university portal.
