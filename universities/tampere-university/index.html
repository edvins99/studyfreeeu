Tuition policy & mandatory fees

Tuition (EU citizens)€0 tuition for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens
Mandatory semester feeBachelor’s/Master’s student union membership is compulsory; budget approximately €80/year (institution-specific 2026/27 amount not yet verified). Healthcare is paid separately to Kela if liable; check doctoral union conditions separately.
Est. mandatory cost / year€150.7

This figure is the mandatory university cost (tuition + compulsory fees), not living costs. Other mandatory fees: Kela healthcare fee is separate from student union membership: €35.35/term in calendar year 2026. Exempt if covered by social security in another EU/EEA country, Switzerland, the UK or EU institutions; citizenship alone is not sufficient. Send Kela the EHIC/GHIC or other required evidence. Doctoral and exchange students are outside this fee scheme. The 2027 rate is not yet verified.

Admissions & entry requirements

Who can apply

A recognised secondary school qualification for a Bachelor’s degree; a relevant Bachelor’s degree for a Master’s. Entry follows Finland admission rules.

Language requirements

English-taught programmes require English proof (commonly IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 92, set per programme); Finnish/Swedish-taught programmes require national language proof.

English test

Proof of English proficiency (e.g. IELTS/TOEFL) for English-taught programmes; threshold set per programme.

Documents generally required 4 items
  • Secondary school certificate + transcript
  • Bachelor’s degree + transcript (for Master’s)
  • Language certificate
  • Passport / ID copy

Application deadlines (typical)

≈ 22 January (joint application, autumn intake)
