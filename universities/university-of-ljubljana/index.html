Admissions & entry requirements

Who can apply

A secondary school leaving certificate giving access to higher education. For a Master’s: a relevant Bachelor’s degree. Enrolment is handled through the national eVŠ portal.

Language requirements

Slovene-taught programmes require Slovene proof; English-taught programmes require English proof.

English test

Proof of English proficiency (e.g. IELTS/TOEFL or equivalent); the exact threshold is set by the programme.

Documents generally required

  • Secondary school certificate
  • Bachelor’s degree + transcript (for Master’s)
  • Language certificate
  • Passport / ID copy

Application deadlines (typical)

Typical: spring enrolment rounds (≈ February–March and later); verify on the eVŠ portal.

How to apply

National portal (eVŠ) and direct portal · Admissions portal →

First-cycle and single-cycle enrolment runs through the national eVŠ application portal.
