# Data audit — 2026-09-30

Goal of the site: universities where **EU citizens can study in English for free or at low cost**.

## Inclusion rule

1. EU/EEA citizens pay **€0 tuition** (mandatory fees shown separately) **or low tuition up to ≈ $3,000 (≈ €2,700) per year**.
2. The institution offers at least one **English-taught full degree** programme.
