# Data audit — 2026-09-30

Goal of the site: universities where **EU citizens can study tuition-free in English**.

## Inclusion rule

1. EU/EEA citizens pay **€0 tuition** (mandatory semester, union, healthcare or registration fees are allowed and shown).
2. The institution offers at least one **English-taught full degree** programme.
