# Targeted official-source review — 14 October 2026

## Scope and result

This is a targeted maintenance review, not a new complete verification of all
326 institutions or all 3,521 programme entries. The previous full expansion
ended on 12 October. This review addresses demonstrable fee/language errors
and the next application cycle in Sweden and Finland.

- Updated 69 institution records: 34 Sweden, 34 Finland and one Danish successor
  institution. Not every field of these records has been reverified.
- Updated the Sweden and Finland country-policy summaries.
- Propagated corrected university-level fee/language policy to 696 programme
  entries. Programme titles, availability, duration and individual deadlines
  remain subject to their existing verification caveats.
- No institutions or programme entries added or deleted; existing IDs and URLs
  retained.

## Sweden

- Removed the incorrect €25 compulsory union-cost estimate from KTH, Linköping,
  Lund, Stockholm, Umeå, Gothenburg and Uppsala. Their optional membership fees
  are excluded from mandatory costs.
- Kept the Chalmers exception: membership is compulsory; Bachelor’s/Master’s
  SEK 280 per semester, doctoral SEK 220, exchange SEK 200. Its indicative annual
  Bachelor’s/Master’s budget is now approximately €50 (SEK 560), not a fixed EUR
  charge.
- Added the 2027 national first-round opening (16 October 2026), application
  deadline (15 January 2027), and separate documentation/fee-exemption deadline
  (1 February 2027). The second round is 15 March–15 April 2027, with fewer
  English-taught options. Explicitly distinguished national rounds from
  programme-specific artistic/direct admission dates.
- Country default is tuition-free without a general compulsory union charge;
  the Chalmers exception is stated.

## Finland

- Corrected the false compulsory-union claim at six UAS institutions:
  Haaga-Helia, LAB, Metropolia, Oamk, TAMK and Turku UAS.
  All 21 listed UAS now consistently distinguish optional union membership from
  conditional healthcare liability.
- Kela 2026 healthcare fee: €35.35/term, €70.70 for two terms. Qualifying social
  security in another EU/EEA state, Switzerland, the UK or EU institutions exempts
  the student. Citizenship alone is not enough. EHIC/GHIC evidence instructions
  are included. Doctoral and exchange students are not in this fee scheme.
- Healthcare is not included in the union fee. Removed the previous misleading
  claim that it was included.
- Verified 2026/27 union membership directly: Aalto €66, Helsinki €85 for
  Bachelor’s/Master’s students. Other university union figures remain labelled
  estimates, not newly institution-verified charges.
- Annual institution budgets assume no healthcare exemption and use two terms
  at the known **2026** rate. This is expressly **not** a confirmed 2026/27 total:
  the spring 2027 Kela rate remains unverified. Students with a qualifying
  exemption exclude the healthcare component. UAS indicative maximum budget is
  €70.70; exempt students owe €0 for this component.
- Added exact 2027 joint-application times and Oulu International Business
  Management’s separate rolling-admission window, without applying that window
  to other degrees. The country page distinguishes document deadlines from
  application deadlines.

## Reproduction and checks

The one-time migration is
`research/2026-10-14-update/apply-update.mjs`, based on main commit `d8e36c9`.
It refuses to rerun on already-migrated data.

Run:

```sh
npm run validate
npm run build
node research/2026-10-14-update/check-update.mjs
```

The regression check covers the corrected union costs and statuses, Kela
exemption wording and exact fee, Aalto/Helsinki union amounts, EK language
flags, inherited programme policies, unchanged record counts and rendered pages.
